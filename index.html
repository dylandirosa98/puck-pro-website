What We Do

Our 3 pillar process takes you from having nothing to send a coach, to a complete recruiting asset in less than a month.

Professional high quality game film, a highlight video that clearly communicates the role you fill on a team, and a player profile that documents your progress and organizes everything into one central link.

01

THE FILM

Our videographers come to your games and capture every shift in 4K — the edgework, zone entries, decision making, compete level — all the small details that coaches actually evaluate.

02

THE HIGHLIGHT VIDEO

Our editors build your recruiting highlight video around your position-specific skillsets. We watch your shifts, identify what makes you valuable, and build a video that shows a coach exactly where you fit on a roster.

03

THE HOCKEY RÉSUMÉ

We tie it all together with your Hockey Résumé — a custom player website that organizes your raw footage, highlight video, academics, statistics, training, progress, and more — built to grow with you over time. A coach can click one link and understand everything they need to know.
