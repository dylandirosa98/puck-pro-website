Before You Decide

What does it cost?

Our standard packages are on our main site — go to puckpromedia.com, select Book Coverage, then Parent/Player. If none of those make sense for your needs and budget, we can customize.

Can we customize a package?

Yes — this is one of the things we can do in real time on the call. We can mix and match services to create a custom package that aligns most with your needs.

How do we pick which games you come to?

Come prepared with your full schedule, or two to three times the number of games you plan on booking. We'll run through your preferences, our availability, and nail down a plan from there.

What if we already have footage and just need editing?

We offer editing services even if you don't need us to film. These jobs are handled on a case by case basis, depending on the amount of footage you have and what we're sorting through.

Do you offer payment plans?

We can break payments up into two to four installments depending on the package. When you receive your invoice, you'll also have the option to finance through Affirm for small monthly payments.

Can we do this with our team or multiple players?

Yes — we create custom packages for team and multi-player scenarios.

What's your turnaround time?

Raw footage is uploaded within 72 hours of game completion, and edited videos are delivered within a week.

What if our player isn't looking to get recruited?

Totally fine — there's more than one use case for video. Whether the player is younger and you just want to document the early days, or you're looking for something fun from each season, we'll create a custom package that makes sense for you.

When should we start?

There's no right or wrong answer. If coaches are already asking for footage, you have plans of applying to schools, or you're making an intentional recruiting push within the next one to two years, those are some signs it's the right time. If you're before that stage, there's still benefit to starting early for tracking progress, reviewing film, and memories' sake.

Where are you located?

Our team currently covers New Jersey, Philadelphia and surrounding areas, Boston and surrounding areas, and parts of Connecticut and New York. We frequently travel outside of these regions for game requests — a travel fee may apply.
