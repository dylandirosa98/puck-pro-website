BECOME A PUCKPRO VIDEOGRAPHER

Looking to join a fast-growing sports media team, build your portfolio, and get paid to film hockey? We're expanding our videographer team and would love to hear from you.

What You'll Do

  • Film live hockey games in a variety of rink settings
  • Capture full games and/or individual players depending on the booking
  • Communicate clearly with clients on-site and with our team remotely
  • Upload raw footage same day — no editing required

Must Have

  • Your own DSLR or mirrorless camera capable of recording 4K video at 60fps
  • Reliable transportation to rinks within an hour radius of your location

Compensation & Benefits

  • Entry-level rate of $75/game once training is complete
  • Clear path to $100, $125, even $150+ per game with experience and priority assignments
  • Flexible freelance schedule — work as much or as little as fits your life
  • Additional opportunities in editing, sales, social media, and other roles as we grow
