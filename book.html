FULL SERVICE PACKAGES

Our two phase system is simple — build it once, keep it fresh. Coaches don't care how many games you've filmed, they care if they can quickly see who your player is and how they play. That's why every plan starts with one of the Season Launch packages below.

After that, most families don't need another huge project, they just need to stay current. Once your Season Launch is complete, you roll into our Recruiting Maintenance Membership — giving you fresh game film, résumé updates, and new clips each month so you're never sending outdated video when a coach reaches out. Big push up front, simple maintenance the rest of the season.
