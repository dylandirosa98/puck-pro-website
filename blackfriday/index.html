What We Do

What we do.

Our videographers come film your games and deliver 4K quality footage — plus a variety of edited content personalized to your use case.

Game Film

All shifts recorded in 4K quality, focused on your player, from a dedicated videographer & camera angle.

Recruiting Highlight Video

3–5 minute highlight video with a custom intro (player info, stats, contact), organized by skill categories with slo-mo, clean transitions, and coach-friendly formatting.

Social Media Shorts

10–20 second highlight clips optimized for social media with text, slo-mo, and music — posted and collaborated with @puckpro_media on Instagram.

Hockey Résumé

A complete digital profile designed to grow with your player season after season — personal website with statistics, academics, training, progress, video, and more.

Hype Video

60–90 second mixtape-style video with best plays from the game, slow-mo, b-roll, transitions, and music of your choice.

Highlight Recap

5 minute video showcasing all the best plays of the game, similar to NHL Highlights on YouTube.
